What the model measures

Performance is contextualised rather than judged by raw averages alone.

1 · Pressure context

Delivery-level pressure index combines innings phase, wickets lost and chase requirements. Threshold 0.48 is the primary scenario; 0.42 and 0.54 test sensitivity.

2 · Phase-matched benchmark

Player outcomes are compared with baseline performance in comparable innings phases, reducing the risk of comparing powerplay output with death-over conditions.

3 · Evidence and shrinkage

Minimum pressure sample and match filters are applied. Uplift measures are shrunk toward the benchmark for smaller samples; match-level benchmark consistency is reported separately.

How to read reliability

Three evidence dimensions are shown for every shortlisted player.

Sample depth

Pressure balls and pressure matches indicate how much evidence supports the score. A high score from a small sample deserves more caution than a similar score supported by many matches.

Threshold stability

Top-10 appearances shows how often a player remains in the top ten across the 0.42, 0.48 and 0.54 scenarios. It is a robustness signal, not a separate skill score.

Match-level consistency

Positive benchmark match share is the proportion of evaluated pressure matches in which the player's match-level metric exceeded the phase-matched benchmark. It is descriptive, not a forecast.

Decision boundary

The final shortlist should combine model evidence with role fit, squad balance, availability, age, injury status, overseas-player constraints and auction price. Those are not established by this dataset alone.

Scope, governance & limitations

Transparent boundaries for responsible use of the analysis.

  • Source scope: 200,999 supplied delivery records; 193,988 eligible rows after competition/innings filters; 837 matches across 136 competitions.
  • 34 matches were flagged as having revised overs in the supplied match data. Revised-innings handling is an approximation where source fields are incomplete.
  • IPL and T20I performances are combined for a broader T20 pressure sample; competition and role context can still differ.
  • Small samples, changing team roles, opposition quality, venue, pitch, match state and scoring conventions can affect rankings.
  • This dashboard contains aggregate player-level analysis only; it does not include or redistribute the raw ball-by-ball data pack.
  • Player dropdown availability does not establish IPL 2027 auction eligibility, retention/release status or actual availability.
